The Process

How We Work

We begin with a discovery call, define your strategy, then bring your website to life.

01

Book a Discovery Call

Let's start with a quick call to get to know your business, goals, and what you're hoping to achieve with your website. This step helps us align expectations and ensures we're the right fit.

02

Share Brief & Present Proposal

Once we've had our discovery call, you'll fill out a short creative brief. Based on that, I'll send over a detailed proposal outlining timelines, scope, and pricing for your review.

03

Start Designing!

Once approved, I'll begin designing your website—sharing updates and collecting feedback along the way to ensure everything aligns with your vision.
